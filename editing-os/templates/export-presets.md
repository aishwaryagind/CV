# Export presets

| Target | Resolution | Codec | Frame rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | 3840x2160 or 1920x1080 | H.264/H.265 | match source | High bitrate, -14 LUFS |
| Reels/Shorts/TikTok | 1080x1920 | H.264 | 30 or 60 | Keep captions in safe area |
| Master/archive | source | ProRes / DNxHR | match source | Keep for re-exports |

Always export a master first, then derive platform versions from it.
